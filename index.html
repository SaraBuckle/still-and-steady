About Still & Steady

Still & Steady is a free, Scripture-centred encouragement space. Little Hearts is designed to be explored with a parent or trusted adult. Our reflections are not a substitute for mental-health care, and faith and professional support can go together.

We don't ask for accounts, names, ages or messages. Favourites are stored on your device only. Browser speech is optional. Read our privacy and safety information.
