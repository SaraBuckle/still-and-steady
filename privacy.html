Privacy & Safety

Still & Steady is a free, static website for Bible-based encouragement. It does not require registration, ask for personal information, or transmit your feelings to our servers.

Saved passages

Favourites are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. You can remove them from the website or clear your browser storage. They are not synced between devices.

Audio and sharing

Optional read-aloud uses your browser's speech features. Available voices and processing vary by device. Sharing and Pinterest links are optional; if you follow them, the third-party service's privacy practices apply. Generated verse cards are created on your device.

Children

Little Hearts is designed for a parent or trusted adult to explore alongside a child. We do not provide unrestricted AI chat, and we do not request children's names or contact details.

Important support information

Scripture and prayers can offer comfort but are not substitutes for mental-health care. If anxiety is affecting everyday life, talk with a trusted person and a qualified healthcare professional. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Scripture

Scripture quotations are from the public-domain King James Version (KJV). Reflections and prayers are original encouragement, not Bible quotations.
